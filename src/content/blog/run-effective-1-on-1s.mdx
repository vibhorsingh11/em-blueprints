## The meeting belongs to them

Let your report set the agenda. Keep a shared running document.

## A simple structure

1. Check in as a person.
2. Their topics first.
3. Feedback and growth.
4. Agree on next steps.

<Callout type="warning">Don't cancel 1:1s casually. They signal priority.</Callout>
