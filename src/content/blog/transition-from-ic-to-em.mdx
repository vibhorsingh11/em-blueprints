## Your output is now the team's output

As an individual contributor, your impact is what you ship. As a manager, it is what your team ships and how
well they grow while doing it.

<Callout type="tip">Spend your first weeks listening more than fixing.</Callout>

## Feedback loops get slower

Code gives feedback in minutes. People take quarters.

### Practical takeaway

Build lightweight rituals, such as weekly 1:1s, so slow loops still get regular signal.
